# Comprehensive Fixes for QuickBook Customer App (Responsive + Functionality)

**Repo:** iSuryaa/QuickBook-Clone  
**Current state:** Mobile‑only UI, stretched on tablet/desktop. Many core flows (category filtering, booking detail, payment, bookings list, profile, waitlist) are non‑functional or missing.  
**Target:** Fully responsive app that works beautifully on mobile (375px), tablet (768px), and desktop (1440px), with a complete user journey from browsing to booking.

---
